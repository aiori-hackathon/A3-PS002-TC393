Show the consent lifecycle

Follow this short sequence during your presentation.

  1. 1Grant a purposeRecord a clear choice for one purpose.
  2. 2Check accessRequest synthetic data and observe ALLOW.
  3. 3Withdraw consentWithdraw for that purpose.
  4. 4Check againThe next request should be denied.
  5. 5Change the noticeShow that the old grant needs a fresh choice.
